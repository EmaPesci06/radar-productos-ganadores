# Prompts - Imagenes con IA

> [!info] Archivo original: [PROMPTS IMAGENES CON IA.docx](file:///C:/Users/WIN-10/Desktop/Ecommerce%20Curso/Modulo%204%20-%20Creativos%20publicitarios/PROMPTS%20IMAGENES%20CON%20IA.docx) · [[Modulo 4 - Creativos publicitarios]]

Prompt para generar con IA una foto realista tipo selfie de una persona sosteniendo el empaque del producto, a partir de una imagen de referencia. Se usa para crear imágenes de creativos publicitarios.

**Se usa en:** [[M4-07 Cómo generar fotos ultra realistas del producto con IA|M4·7 Cómo generar fotos ultra realistas del producto con IA]]

```text
A realistic 9:16 vertical photograph of a person holding the specific product packaging from reference image 1. The person is using the camera-facing side of a modern smartphone, capturing a high-quality selfie. They are smiling, making natural eye contact with the phone’s lens. The main hand is holding the product clearly toward the camera, ensuring all labels and packaging details from reference image 1 are sharp and visible. The other hand may be holding the phone. The background is a gently blurred, bright, and clean modern room or outdoor setting, focusing all attention on the person and the product. Soft, flattering natural lighting.
```
