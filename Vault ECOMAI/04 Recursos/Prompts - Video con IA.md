# Prompts - Video con IA

> [!info] Archivo original: [PROMPTS VIDEO IA.docx](file:///C:/Users/WIN-10/Desktop/Ecommerce%20Curso/Modulo%204%20-%20Creativos%20publicitarios/PROMPTS%20VIDEO%20IA.docx) · [[Modulo 4 - Creativos publicitarios]]

Dos prompts para animar con IA una imagen de referencia en un video corto y realista: uno de movimiento natural sutil y otro de tres escenas (selfie, apertura del producto y uso). Se usan para generar creativos en video.

**Se usa en:** [[M4-11 Creativos en video con IA para gadgets|M4·11 Creativos en video con IA para gadgets]] · [[M4-12 Creativos en video con IA para indumentaria|M4·12 Creativos en video con IA para indumentaria]] · [[M4-13 Proceso completo de creativos con IA|M4·13 Proceso completo de creativos con IA]]

## Prompt 1 — Video corto con movimiento natural (a partir de una imagen de referencia)

```text
A high-quality, photorealistic short video clip based on the reference image(s). The model is naturally holding and interacting with the product. The animation must be subtle, lifelike, and highly realistic. Specific motion requirements: 1. Face: The model blinks softly and naturally once or twice. There is a very slight, slow tilt or turn of the head (micro-movement), maintaining focus near the camera or product. 2. Body: Gentle, subtle rise and fall of the chest indicating natural breathing. 3. Hands: The hands holding the product remain mostly steady, with only minute, realistic shifts in grip or tension. The product itself does not morph or distort. Maintain consistent cinematographic lighting and high fidelity to the original image's texture and reality. No sudden movements, no exaggerated expressions, and no warping. The video should feel calm and professional. 4k resolution.
```

## Prompt 2 — Video de 3 escenas (selfie, apertura del producto y cápsula)

```text
A continuous 9:16 vertical video containing three seamless scenes featuring the person from [persona.jpg] and the exact product packaging from [producto.jpg].
Scene 1: The person is holding the product from [producto.jpg], smiling warmly, and gently presenting the product toward the camera in a selfie-style angle with smooth, soft movement.
Scene 2: A close-up shot of the person's hands carefully opening the exact product packaging.
Scene 3: The person takes a single capsule out of the product packaging.
The product packaging must remain 100% identical to [producto.jpg] in every scene. The video style is clean, modern, and realistic with soft, flattering lighting.
```
